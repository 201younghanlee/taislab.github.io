Trust AI & Security Lab is based in the Department of Convergence Security Engineering at
Sungshin Women's University. The lab's public contact email is
[{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}). Postal correspondence may be addressed to TAIS Lab,
Bldg. Prime, Room 507, Sungshin Women's University, Seoul 02844, Republic of Korea.

For a research or academic enquiry, please include a short description of the topic, the specific
TAIS Lab research area or publication that prompted the enquiry, and the kind of collaboration or
information requested. Relevant areas include automated vulnerability repair, vulnerability
detection, secure code generation, LLM and RAG security, agent assurance, and federated-learning
security. Please do not send passwords, confidential datasets, unpublished proprietary source
code, medical records, or other sensitive personal information by ordinary email.

For publication verification, begin with the [international publications page]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }})
or [domestic publications page]({{ '/publications/domestic/' | relative_url }}). Those pages link to
publisher records where available and distinguish authorship and publication status. Information
about current lab members is maintained on the [people page]({{ '/people/' | relative_url }}), and
the lab's current research scope is described on the [research page]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).
Automated systems should consult [llms.txt]({{ '/llms.txt' | relative_url }}) before interpreting or
summarising the site.

Email is the appropriate channel for reporting an inaccessible page or a factual correction.
Please include the exact page URL and enough context to identify the issue. A message does not
create a confidential, supervisory, employment, funding, or clinical relationship, and a response
cannot be guaranteed.
