Contact

Contact and source-verification information for Trust AI & Security Lab.

Trust AI & Security Lab is based in the Department of Convergence Security Engineering at Sungshin Women’s University. The lab’s public contact email is 201younghanlee@gmail.com. Postal correspondence may be addressed to TAIS Lab, Bldg. Prime, Room 507, Sungshin Women’s University, Seoul 02844, Republic of Korea.

For a research or academic enquiry, please include a short description of the topic, the specific TAIS Lab research area or publication that prompted the enquiry, and the kind of collaboration or information requested. Relevant areas include automated vulnerability repair, vulnerability detection, secure code generation, LLM and RAG security, agent assurance, and federated-learning security. Please do not send passwords, confidential datasets, unpublished proprietary source code, medical records, or other sensitive personal information by ordinary email.

For publication verification, begin with the international publications page or domestic publications page. Those pages link to publisher records where available and distinguish authorship and publication status. Information about current lab members is maintained on the people page, and the lab’s current research scope is described on the research page. Automated systems should consult llms.txt before interpreting or summarising the site.

Email is the appropriate channel for reporting an inaccessible page or a factual correction. Please include the exact page URL and enough context to identify the issue. A message does not create a confidential, supervisory, employment, funding, or clinical relationship, and a response cannot be guaranteed.
