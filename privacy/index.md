# Privacy

Privacy information for visitors to the Trust AI & Security Lab website.

This privacy notice describes the public TAIS Lab website at `www.taislab.co.kr`. The site is an informational academic website. It does not provide user accounts, payment facilities, comment forms, or a first-party contact form, and TAIS Lab does not ask visitors to submit personal data through the website. If you choose to contact the lab by email, the information in your message is used to read and respond to the enquiry and to maintain any necessary academic or administrative record of that correspondence.

The website is built and published using GitHub infrastructure and may be delivered through GitHub Pages and an edge or content-delivery provider. Like most web hosts, these hosting and network providers may process technical request information such as an IP address, request time, requested URL, browser or user-agent details, and error information for security, reliability, and operational logging. TAIS Lab does not currently configure Google Analytics or Cronitor analytics on this site. The colour-theme control stores a preference in the visitor’s browser using local storage so that the selected appearance can be restored; it is not used to identify the visitor.

Some pages load resources from third-party content-delivery networks and publication services. Publication pages may display Altmetric or Dimensions information, and external links may lead to publishers, ORCID, Google Scholar, GitHub, LinkedIn, or other independent services. Requests to those services are governed by their own privacy terms and may disclose ordinary connection data to them. Visitors should review an external service’s policy before supplying information there.

Do not email confidential research data, passwords, health information, or other sensitive personal information unless an appropriate secure method has first been agreed.
